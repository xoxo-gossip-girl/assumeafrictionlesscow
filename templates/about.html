About Us

As CGP Grey once said, 'Leaving the real world: with its class system and people, and untidiness and smells, ascending to the platonic plain of physics, where cows are spheres and people are cellular automaton who will execute instructions perfectly… witnessing such glory is too much for mortal minds to bear for long so we are forced to descend back down into the meat world. The cacophony! The smell!'


If only this were true. We are a group of A-level STEM (as in only Maths, Further Maths, Physics, Chemistry) students who wish to share our love of physics and maths with others, so that our Physics and Maths Society presentations can forever be immortalised.

Side note: to clarify, we do accept biology as an equal member of the STEM clan and did not attempt to offend any persons with our so called 'biology slander.' We have many friends who take biology, and they are, on the whole, very nice people.
